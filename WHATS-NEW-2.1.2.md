# What's new in The AnkiDote 2.1.2

A small release. Two things: a tidier Settings dialog, and the rest of
the Australian drug names that 2.1.1 started on.

## Settings is quieter

The three module switches are now three plain checkboxes. The captions
underneath two of them said what the checkbox label already said, so
they have gone.

## The rest of the Australian drug names

2.1.1 fixed twenty-three drug spellings that had been matching nothing.
That list was assembled from names that happened to come up. This one is
taken from the TGA's own list of updated ingredient names, which turned
out to have twelve more rows where the old spelling matched nothing and
the new one was already in the database:

`benzhexol`, `flupenthixol`, `dexamphetamine`, `hydroxyurea`,
`eformoterol`, `glycopyrrolate`, `chlorpheniramine`, `cholestyramine`,
`clomiphene`, `ethinyloestradiol`, `actinomycin D`.

Textbooks, older lecture slides and ward protocols still use the
left-hand form freely, so a card written from any of them got no popup
at all.

## Benztropine is now benzatropine

The Australian Approved Name is benzatropine, and the database held only
the American spelling, so the popup heading was showing the American
name on every acute dystonia and drug-induced parkinsonism card. Cards
written the American way still work; they just show the Australian name
now.

---

**Upgrading:** nothing to do. Your settings, shortcuts and dock layout
carry over untouched.
